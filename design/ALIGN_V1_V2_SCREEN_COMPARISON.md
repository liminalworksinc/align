# Align v1 → v2: screen-by-screen design comparison

Design review · October 30, 2026 · LIM-2449 / PR #34

**Target: v2.** [Founder ruling LIM-2454, e3a14f87](https://linear.app/liminal-space-io/issue/LIM-2454#comment-e3a14f87) supersedes v1 as the implementation target. Separate leadership launch authorization and **Accept if…** conditions remain **required additions** before any design is cleared.

**Evidence:** v1 source at `e044f11a9fca5b12713beed68df05a4348e9f675` (the founder's Fly v17 baseline); v2 specs and selected implementation at `237268f058cafc2f2a3b3b9cb51352b82bf88241`. This compares the live-v1 source contract with the pinned v2 design contract, not two verified running builds. All “clearer,” priority and port/redesign choices below are **design inferences**, not independent usability results. No deployment was tested.

## Recommendation

**Use v2's identity, goals, approval sets, revisions and evidence as the product model. Build a unified, recognition-first Home with both founder-selected jobs: affected work and decisions needing attention.** Then converge them on one change-request review and one inspectable decision record. Retain the useful v1 interaction patterns; do not copy its fixed three-role state machine, repeated summaries or exact layout.

The screen choices in the table are proposals, not founder clearance. Use v2 as specified for **sign-in/account, People/invites, core goal/version permissions, Sessions controls, Spend calculations, Asks actions, OAuth consent and value-report permissions**. Redesign how first-time operators encounter those capabilities.

### What is actually dropped or changed

1. **Separate leadership launch — absent and REQUIRED.** The v2 state/action vocabulary has no launch decision. Approval already sends new goal versions to bound sessions. The cleared design must record leadership's separate decision, actor, time and note and visibly distinguish not decided, not yet and approved. Specify what a held launch authorizes or prevents relative to goal-context delivery; neither the specs nor v1's UI establishes that the launch decision automatically stops all work. Session Stop/Resume is not a substitute.
2. **Accept if… — absent and REQUIRED.** V2 counters replace goal fields and carry a note. A note, an evidence link and a goal limit are not v1's separate conditions. Preserve a condition-only counter that keeps the proposed target, requester acceptance, subsequent approval by an eligible approver and persistent **Not confirmed yet** until confirmation has its own evidence. V1 itself stores these terms but does not check fulfilment; do not imply that accepting or approving verifies them.
3. **AI intake interviewer — absent from the pinned v2 screen/route contract.** Pasted-note limit suggestions are a narrower operation, not an intake interview. Restore the useful draft/review interaction against v2 objects if desired, without requiring a fictional two-team conflict to connect existing work.
4. **Timer — changed, not “no measurement.”** V1's coordination clock starts when the company change is viewed and ends when both team roles have seen the accepted version; it retains touchpoints and excluded preparation. Its value card also separately measures proposal→agreement and proposal→launch. V2 keeps proposal→approval and adds approval→first applied receipt, but does not specify the both-human-re-entry clock or launch duration. Label each metric by its actual endpoints; the old and new coordination times are not interchangeable.
5. **Waitlist/LOI funnel — replaced, not deleted.** V2 already has the value card, estimate, view/report/follow-up events, access/evaluation request and operator-recorded LOIs. It drops the two distinct v1 waitlist/LOI CTAs and their click-event vocabulary in favour of one configurable follow-up. The default request-access page explicitly asks for no company signature. Retaining the existing direct waitlist/form conversion requires a deliberate destination and event mapping.
6. **Other v1 affordances not specified in the same form:** a unified Your turn and dedicated Decisions destination; the required reciprocal-commitment field; the generic declared-work/old-plan inventory; and Apps' whole-plan read-freshness flag. Preserve the operator jobs where useful, but do not invent v2 fields. In particular, v2's receipt-backed session status is not the same measurement as an OAuth app's last read.

### What v2 adds—and what matters for an independent multi-agent tester

- **Named people, teams and email-bound invites** replace scenario roles and shared passcodes. Goal consent is **one eligible approver from that goal's effective approval set**; alignments can have several affected-goal/session-owner requirements, **each** needing consent. Team membership does not grant every permission. These are necessary infrastructure for real collaboration, not proof of first-run value.
- **An ordinary, self-owned Northstar sample** makes a solo approval exercise possible without switching identities. V1 already has a local Northstar demo; the addition is the personal workspace/hosted-v2 path. The sample's self-owned request is specially seeded and is not evidence that normal users may submit change requests to their own goals or independently validate multi-person approval. Hosted creation gating can hide the sample affordance.
- **Shared Sessions, bindings, directives and receipts** provide the strongest fit for a tester trying to understand several agents' work. This is a **fit inference**, not participant research: the provided code/specs cannot establish what a named tester actually requested or successfully used. Connecting an MCP app does not replace CLI/device setup, session sharing and goal binding; idle agents are not awakened.
- **Spend** adds usage and estimated list cost by goal/team/own session. **Asks** adds a dedicated, permissioned team-request workflow. Asks are not entirely new in code: v1 already contains local ask cards, while its hosted write routes are closed. Neither Spend nor Asks should be claimed as a validated need of that multi-agent tester from these sources.
- **Evidence links, immutable per-revision snapshots, withdraw/rebase and parent-review states** strengthen review. Snapshots record bounded spend/session/activity facts; links are self- or agent-reported and explicitly unverified. These help review consequential changes, but neither a link nor an applied receipt establishes work completion or business success.

**Order the design work:** first v2 onboarding plus unified Home; then change-request review with required conditions and launch; then decision record and affected work; then Apps and the post-decision conversion path. Review each against the private D0 originals and rendered v2 screens before claiming visual or independent-test superiority. This task changes design documentation only; it grants no implementation, merge or deployment clearance.
